Foods for Healthy Hair
For healthy hair, focus on adequate protein, iron, zinc, omega-3 fats, vitamin C, vitamin D, B vitamins, and overall adequate calories. No single food can guarantee faster hair growth, but a balanced diet supports normal hair growth and strength.

----Protein-rich foods

Hair is primarily made of keratin, a protein.

Eggs
Paneer
Curd/Greek yogurt
Milk
Dal and lentils
Chickpeas
Rajma
Soybeans/tofu
Peanuts
Pumpkin seeds


----Iron-rich foods

Iron is important for oxygen transport and normal hair follicle function.

Spinach and other leafy greens
Lentils
Chickpeas
Rajma
Soybeans
Pumpkin seeds
Sesame seeds
Dates and raisins

Tip: Pair plant-based iron sources with vitamin-C-rich foods such as amla, guava, oranges, lemon, or bell peppers to improve iron absorption.

For a vegetarian diet:

Chia seeds

---- B12 & vitamin D

These can be harder to obtain from a vegetarian diet.

Milk and curd
Eggs
Fortified foods
Fortified plant milks/cereals

Vitamin B12 is particularly important if your diet contains little or no animal food.

----Easy daily combination

A simple hair-supportive day could look like:

Breakfast: Oats + milk/curd + banana + chia/flaxseed + a few nuts
Lunch: Rice/roti + dal + leafy vegetable + curd + lemon
Snack: Guava/amla/orange + roasted chana
Dinner: Paneer/tofu/soy + vegetables + roti
Optional: Egg if you eat eggs

And don't forget water and sufficient overall food intake—extreme calorie restriction can itself negatively affect hair.

If you're specifically trying to grow longer, thicker hair, nutrition is only one part; sleep, scalp care, hair handling, and identifying deficiencies can matter too.
