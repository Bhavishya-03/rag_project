FRUIT NUTRITION REFERENCE
Nutritional facts per 100 g of edible portion
========================================================================

This long text document contains approximate nutritional information for
commonly consumed fruits. Values are intended for general reference and
for testing a Python text-file parser. Actual values vary by variety,
ripeness, growing conditions, storage, and database source.

FIELDS: Calories (kcal), Carbohydrates (g), Protein (g), Fat (g),
Dietary Fiber (g), Sugars (g), and Water (g).

IMPORTANT: These are approximate values for raw edible fruit unless
otherwise stated. They are not individualized medical or dietary advice.
========================================================================

========================================================================
GENERAL OBSERVATIONS
========================================================================

Lower-calorie, water-rich examples include watermelon, lemon, lime,
strawberry, cantaloupe, honeydew, pomelo, peach, and grapefruit.

Some fruits are considerably more calorie-dense. Coconut flesh and
avocado are notable because they contain much more fat than most fruits.
Fresh dates, passion fruit, custard apple, and jackfruit also provide
more calories per 100 g than many water-rich fruits.

Fruits such as passion fruit, raspberry, avocado, guava, blackberry,
and pomegranate provide relatively substantial dietary fiber.

Fresh and dried fruit should not be compared directly by weight because
drying removes water and concentrates calories and sugars.

Whole fruit and fruit juice can also differ because juicing may remove
much of the fiber and makes it easier to consume larger quantities quickly.

========================================================================
DATA QUALITY NOTES
========================================================================

1. Nutritional composition naturally varies between fruits.
2. Different food databases may report slightly different numbers.
3. Ripeness can change water, sugar, and carbohydrate levels.
4. Values are per 100 g, not per fruit or per serving.
5. Edible portion is used; peel, seeds, and pits are excluded when
   they are normally not consumed.
6. Added sugar is not included in raw whole-fruit values.
7. This document is for general reference and software testing.
